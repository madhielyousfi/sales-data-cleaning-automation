# Freelance Customer Pitch — Sales Data Cleaning

## Service Offer

I build reusable data-cleaning pipelines that convert messy e-commerce sales exports into reliable, analytics-ready datasets.

## Typical Customer Pain Points

- Duplicate orders across Shopify, WooCommerce, and manual Excel files
- Refunds and cancellations inflating revenue reports
- Inconsistent product categories breaking cohort analysis
- Malformed emails, bad dates, and invalid prices wasting analyst time

## What the Customer Receives

1. **Reusable Python CLI** that accepts their raw CSV/Excel exports.
2. **cleaned_sales.csv / .xlsx** — ready for BI tools or further analysis.
3. **quarantined_sales.csv / .xlsx** — bad records with explicit reasons for manual review.
4. **data_quality_report.md + .json** — defect counts and business impact.
5. **summary_metrics.csv** — monthly sales, revenue by category/country, top products, AOV.
6. **Source code, tests, and documentation** they can run and extend.

## Example Use Case

A small e-commerce brand merges monthly sales from three channels. Before, an analyst spent 4 hours cleaning the file each month. After the pipeline, the same work runs in seconds and is fully reproducible.

## Pricing Guidance

- **Initial build:** Fixed price based on complexity (number of source formats, cleaning rules, reporting needs).
- **Monthly retainer:** Re-run the pipeline on new data and deliver updated reports.

## Value Proposition

- Reduce manual cleanup time by 90%+.
- Improve reporting accuracy with audit trails.
- Provide stakeholders with clean data and clear quality metrics.
