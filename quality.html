Rule reference

Dates

Valid range 2010-01-01 → today. Anything else is quarantined before analytics.

Amounts

quantity > 0 and unit_price > 0, strictly. Discounts coerced to 0.0–0.80.

Identity

Missing order / product / customer IDs quarantined. Bad emails flagged, kept.

Duplicates

order_id + product_id + order_date kept once; repeats quarantined.

Revenue flag

canceled / refunded rows are kept but excluded from realized revenue.

Totals

total_amount = quantity × unit_price × (1 − discount), rounded to cents.
